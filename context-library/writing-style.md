# Writing style

Use this as a living guide for how you (as a design leader) write.

## Default format
- Lead with a 1–2 sentence TL;DR
- Then 3–6 bullets: decision, rationale, trade-offs, next steps

## Tone
- Direct, calm, specific
- Avoid jargon unless needed

## Preference cues
- When writing to execs: crisp ask + risk posture + timing
- When writing to eng: constraints, edge cases, acceptance criteria
- When writing to design: principles, rationale, quality bar, examples
