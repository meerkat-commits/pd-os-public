Venmo Experience Vision

Head of Design, Venmo Nov 2023 – Jul 2024 SLT-approved vision

Stakes

Venmo owned P2P social payments. The 2024 bet was bigger: build a cohesive target experience that moved OKRs forward without losing what makes Venmo feel like Venmo.

The product had to go from splitting bills with friends to building financial confidence across wallet, local commerce, and everyday money management in one trusted app.

The call

From splitting bills to building financial confidence. Take Venmo Anywhere.
  • Intention: Develop a cohesive target experience for Venmo that drives progress towards OKRs.
  • Mood: Fun, inclusive, easy, lightweight, safe.
  • Repositioning: Single-use → multi-use app. P2P social core plus Wallet, Anywhere, and commerce flywheel.
  • Prioritization: Gap matrix on Simple / Local / Social / Enriching with explicit P1–P4 focus areas.

Value props

PropWhat it meant
Celebrate our customers Real-life moments (moving day, brunch, friendship), not abstract fintech
Make it simple and intuitive Core flows and payment method selection stay effortless
Lead with the Venmo spirit Social, warm, human. The brand is the experience

Trade-offs

  • Principles over pattern-matching. Benchmarked Cash App, Revolut, and others for experience principles. Not surface copy-paste.
  • Vision before feature sprawl. Gap matrix forced priority calls instead of shipping every wallet/commerce idea at once.
  • SLT gate, not design-only deck. Consumer leads and directors reviewed before OKR sequencing.
  • Multi-use without losing P2P soul. Anywhere and Wallet extend Venmo; they don't replace the social graph.

Proof

  • SLT approval on vision journey and principles (Mar 2024)
  • Sequenced into 2024 priorities and OKRs
  • Commerce flywheel model: Reach → Customer base → Local sellers → Relevancy, driven by Promo → Personalization → Social proof → Geo-targeting
  • Connected downstream work: Wallet UI, payment construct / debit onboarding, consumer graph validation
