## Philosophy

> Narrative over artifacts. Shared understanding beats another deliverable.  
> AI-native builders at scale. Hub skills, triad gates, and context systems so design, partners, and eng ship from the same playbook.  
> Discipline owners gate craft and intent. Anyone generates with AI; the proof bar is what ships and what users feel.  
> Proof has a bar. A test counts if it moved the product. A prototype counts if Eng can build from it.
