# About

## Short bio

I'm Director of Mobile & AI UX at Mozilla. I lead Firefox's 2026 company-priority work across a 35-person hybrid organization.

The bets I own: Project Nova (full-browser redesign, in Nightly), Smart Window (opt-in agentic AI), AI-native org transformation (operating system, triad gates, proof bars), and design partnership on PLG and monetization.

Day to day I work in Cursor and Claude (Design, Code, Co-work), maintain context in PD-OS, point agents at the [team skills hub](https://github.com/FirefoxUX/ai-native-knowledge-hub), and prototype in the Firefox tree when eng needs something buildable. Anyone on the team can generate with AI. Discipline owners gate craft and intent. The goal is AI-native builders at scale — hub skills, context, and code in the tree.

I write on [Yes + And](https://brookekatalinich.substack.com/) and co-teach [AI-Driven Design on Maven](https://maven.com/bkatalinich/ai-driven-design/preview/354376) with Eduardo Feo.

Full tool stack and Firefox bets: [`ai-native.md`](ai-native.md)
