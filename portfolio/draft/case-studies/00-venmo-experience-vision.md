# Case study: Venmo Experience Vision (2024)

Role: Head of Design, Venmo · PayPal  
Timeline: Nov 2023 – Jul 2024 (Experience Vision SLT approval Mar 2024)  
Scope: Cross-functional vision for Venmo's single-use to multi-use app strategy  
Source deck: `Portfolio 2026.fig` · Venmo page

---

## Stakes

Venmo owned P2P social payments. The 2024 bet was bigger: build a cohesive target experience that moved OKRs forward without losing what makes Venmo feel like Venmo.

The product had to go from splitting bills with friends to building financial confidence across wallet, local commerce, and everyday money management in one trusted app.

---

## The call

### North star

> From splitting bills to building financial confidence. Take Venmo Anywhere.

### Intention

> In 2024, we will develop a cohesive target experience for Venmo that drives progress towards our OKRs.

### Experience mood (Magic)

> Fun, inclusive, easy, lightweight, safe.

### Value props

| Prop | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Celebrate our customers | Real life moments (moving day, brunch, friendship), not abstract fintech |
| Make it simple and intuitive | Payment method selection and core flows stay effortless |
| Lead with the Venmo spirit | Social, warm, human. The brand is the experience |

### Strategic repositioning

Single-use to multi-use app strategy. Venmo began by revolutionizing shared expenses. The vision extended that into:

- Venmo Anywhere: pay and be paid beyond your social graph (local businesses, markets, artists)
- Venmo Wallet: one place for balance, linked accounts, cards, and financial exploration
- Commerce flywheel: promos, personalization, social proof, and geo-targeting feeding reach and local seller growth

### Prioritization (gap matrix)

Experience opportunities mapped on Simple to Enriching and Local to Social, with explicit P1 to P4 priority and focus-area tagging. The vision was a sequencing tool for 2024 work, not a mood board.

---

## Trade-offs

- Principles over pattern-matching. Benchmarked Cash App, Revolut, and others for experience principles. Not surface copy-paste.
- Vision before feature sprawl. Gap matrix forced priority calls (P1 focus areas) instead of shipping every wallet/commerce idea at once.
- SLT gate, not design-only deck. Consumer leads and directors reviewed. Vision had to earn leadership approval before sequencing into OKRs.
- Multi-use without losing P2P soul. Anywhere and Wallet extend Venmo. They don't replace the social feed and friend graph.

---

## Proof

- SLT approval on vision journey and principles (Mar 2024)
- Sequenced into 2024 priorities and OKRs
- Flywheel model adopted for commerce: inner loop (Reach, Customer base, Local sellers, Relevancy) + outer loop (Promo, Personalization, Social proof, Geo-targeting)
- Downstream workstreams connected: Wallet UI, payment construct / debit onboarding, consumer graph validation
