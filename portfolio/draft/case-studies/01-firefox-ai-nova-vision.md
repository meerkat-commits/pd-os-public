Firefox's 2026 bet is two parallel acts in one product. Nova remakes the window. Smart Window makes AI opt-in, private, and worth switching for on Mozilla's terms, not a chatbot stapled to chrome.

## Stakes

> Browsers are awash in a sea of sameness.

Firefox is for people who want protection without compromise, control without complexity, and real choice.

Sameness isn't just visual. Every competitor is bolting on AI. The risk for Firefox isn't being late. It's shipping intelligence that violates trust, or shipping a redesign that ignores why people switch.

> We're designing for people who need a reason to switch.

---

## The call: two bets, one standard

### Bet 1: Nova (reimagine the window, 2026+)

Youthful, fresh, built around authentic moments of delight.

Project Nova is the company-priority redesign: chrome, new tab, onboarding, settings. In Nightly, in public. Not churn for its own sake. Cohesion and empowerment after Proton.

Nova answers: Does Firefox feel like it belongs in 2026 and still feel like home?

- Lavender-to-peach tab strip, soft pill geometry, compact mode in Appearance settings
- Parallel modes in chrome. Classic and Smart Window share token discipline; they're not separate products
- Community-in-the-loop: Reddit-in-thread replies, design principles as shared UX/Product/Eng standard
- Token trajectory: NTP propagation 28% to 77.5%; Smart Window greenfield at 100% font-size tokenized

### Bet 2: Smart Window (AI on Mozilla's terms, 2025 to 2026)

Personalized, private, always there when you need it.

AI is not a takeover. Three modes, one product:

| Mode | Frame |
|------|-------|
| Private | On-device, maximum privacy |
| Classic | Familiar Firefox; light intelligence you can ignore |
| Smart Window / AI Mode | Opt-in, context-aware assistant. The browser carries more of the task, with consent |

The Agentic pillar from mobile vision maps here: conversational search, follow-up threads, task completion inside the window, not a detached chat app.

> Browse faster. Privately. On your terms.

### One craft standard for both

Nova and Smart Window are not sequential handoffs. Smart Window inherits Nova tokens from day one. Chrome decisions (switcher placement, app menu consistency across window types) apply to Classic, Smart, and Private alike.

Design the states nobody sees (loading, error, empty, reduced-motion, the moment after the user turns AI off) with the same care as Nova's hero chrome.

## How we got there

### Design craft pillars (FF deck)

1. Target experience: define the best experience given the timeline; work customer-back across coordinated releases
2. Visual refinement: teardowns, Gestalt principles, analogous and unrelated problem spaces
3. Iterative process: validate hypotheses across the full lifecycle; bring work in early and often

### H1 workstreams (mobile to desktop bridge)

| Workstream | Activities | Milestone |
|------------|------------|-----------|
| Polish key surfaces | Onboarding · Toolbar · Tab management · NTP | Eng handoff to target launch |
| Benchmarking | Product teardowns · UI audit | Complete |
| Vision journey and principles | Storyboard · Sequence into priorities · Design system impact | SLT approval |

Footnote from deck: initial refresh leveraged hard-coding and existing tokens. Vision work built on tokens with the system team. Same arc as Nova AWDTY scorecard.

### Validation flywheel

Identify problem → Scope → Hypothesis → Experiment → Release → Measure → Iterate

Plus: usability testing, A/B and multi-variant testing, quarterly live audits, NPS / HEART metrics.

## Trade-offs

- AI opt-in, not AI default. Classic stays Firefox. Smart Window is for when you want it.
- Nova ships chrome before every AI surface is eng-ready. Product Review 1 / 2 gates; not everything is prototype-complete on day one.
- Motion scope cut where it didn't earn its place. Reload/stop animation removed; bookmark animation deferred post-Nova.
- Community promises stay scoped. Roundness customization explored in-thread. Not shipped in Settings until ready.
- Question borrowed patterns. Competitor AI curves aren't decisions. Greenfield token discipline on Smart Window (100% font-size) sets the bar.
